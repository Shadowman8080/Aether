# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Aether — issues, pull requests, discussions and any
community space — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information — a physical or email address, for
  example — without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Why this project is stricter than usual

Aether is security-adjacent software that makes explicit claims about privacy
and isolation. That context shapes the moderation bar:

- **Do not pressure anyone into dropping a caveat.** A maintainer who documents
  a limitation is doing the project a favour. Pushing them to overstate what has
  been verified is harassment, even if phrased politely.
- **No credential handling in public.** If you post a key, token or password,
  expect it to be treated as compromised and rotated immediately.
- **Disagree with the technical claim, not the person.** "That assertion is
  wrong because X" is welcome. Personal attacks are not.
- **Speculation presented as fact causes real harm.** "This must be secure"
  because it should be secure is a safety issue here, not a style issue.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces — the issue tracker, pull requests,
discussions — and also when an individual is officially representing the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour through a **private** channel with the maintainer
rather than a public issue.

Reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer will
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit or reject content
that is not aligned with this code, and to temporarily or permanently ban any
participant for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive
or harmful.
